Privacy

When you book a slot on this website we collect only what the booking needs:

  • your name and email address from your Google account (when you choose "Continue with Google");
  • the phone number you give us;
  • the bKash number you paid the advance from, and the bKash transaction ID.

We use them to check your payment, hold your slot and email you when your booking is confirmed or can't be confirmed. Only the ground's staff see them. We don't sell or share them, and we don't send marketing emails.

We never see your Google password, and we only ask Google for your name and email address.

To ask about your data or have it deleted, email tturfzone@gmail.com.
