Good to know

Is the advance refunded?

No. The ৳{{ advance }} advance is not refunded if you cancel, so choose your hour with care.

When is it confirmed?

When the ground has checked your bKash payment. You get an email either way.

What date is a 1 AM game?

The next calendar date. Night runs from 6 PM to 6 AM, and the booking shows the real date.

Why sign in with Google?

So the confirmation email reaches you. Your name and email are used for your booking only.

One TrxID, two hours?

No. Each bKash TrxID pays for one booking. For two hours, make two bookings.
